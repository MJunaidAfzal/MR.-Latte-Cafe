Who We Are

A Neighborhood Gem

MR. Latte is a small, friendly café with a big menu — coffee and espresso, signature drinks, matcha, sandwiches, salads, ice cream and desserts.

Our drinks draw on flavors from across the Americas — Belize, Guatemala, the Dominican Republic — and our sandwiches are piled high and made fresh. Guests say the owner makes everyone feel welcome and is happy to take special requests.
